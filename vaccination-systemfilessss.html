EmailJS Setup Guide (5 minutes, free)
  1. Go to emailjs.com and create a free account
  2. Click Email Services → Add New Service (choose Gmail, Outlook, etc.) → copy the Service ID
  3. Click Email Templates → Create New Template. Use these variables in your template body:
    {{to_email}}   {{guardian_name}}   {{child_name}}   {{vaccine_name}}   {{due_date}}   {{clinic_name}}
  4. Copy the Template ID
  5. Go to Account → copy your Public Key
  6. Paste all three above and click Save Config
